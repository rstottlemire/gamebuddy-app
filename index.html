How to Install
You’re using the installed app. You’re all set!

Installing puts GameBuddy on your home screen like a regular app. It opens full-screen and works offline, even in airplane mode.

  1. Open the GameBuddy link in Safari. iPhone can only install from Safari, not from Chrome or the link preview in Messages.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (at the top on iPad).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen .
  4. Tap Add in the top-right corner. GameBuddy appears on your home screen. Open it from there.
  1. Open the GameBuddy link in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app below if it shows up. Otherwise tap the menu in the top-right corner, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install to confirm. GameBuddy appears on your home screen and in your app drawer.

Open GameBuddy once while you’re online after installing so it can save itself for offline play. Updates download automatically the next time you open it with a connection.
